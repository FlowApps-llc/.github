# Getting help

Our repositories are private, so GitHub issues are for the FlowApps team. If you use one of our
products and need help, email us instead:

- **Product help, billing and account questions:** [contact@flowapps.me](mailto:contact@flowapps.me).
  Say which product you use (FlowApps, Emailify or TodoHub) and, for an app, the version shown in
  its settings.
- **Security problems:** see our [security policy](./SECURITY.md). Please do not post them publicly.
- **Privacy and your data:** see our [privacy policy](https://flowapps.me/privacy), or email us to
  ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

We reply in Arabic or English.
