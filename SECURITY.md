# Security policy

This policy covers everything FlowApps OÜ builds and runs: the FlowApps platform, our mobile apps
(Emailify, TodoHub), our websites under `flowapps.me`, and the code in this GitHub organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.**

Email **[contact@flowapps.me](mailto:contact@flowapps.me)** with the subject line starting
`[SECURITY]`, and include:

- the product, URL or app version affected;
- what an attacker could do (the impact);
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- any accounts, devices or settings needed to reproduce it.

If the report contains another person's data, send only what is needed to show the problem.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We tell you whether we can reproduce it, and keep you updated while we work on a fix.
- We agree a disclosure date with you once a fix is available. Please give us a reasonable chance
  to fix the issue before you publish anything.
- If you would like to be credited, tell us the name to use.

We do not currently run a paid bug bounty.

## Good-faith research

We will not take legal action against research that:

- stays within this policy;
- uses only accounts and data you own or have permission to use;
- does not degrade our services (no denial-of-service, spam or social engineering of our staff
  or customers);
- stops and reports as soon as you reach someone else's data.

## Out of scope

- Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
- Missing security headers or best-practice suggestions with no exploitable issue.
- Vulnerabilities in third-party services we use (report those to the vendor), unless our
  configuration causes them.
